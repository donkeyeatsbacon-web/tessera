Tessera

Every word a tile. Every poem a mosaic.

pasteanytext,rearrangethewords

Tessera is a free tool for making found poetry. Paste anything (a sonnet, a news story, a recipe, a weather forecast) and it becomes a pile of word tiles. Drag them onto the canvas, in any order, and a new poem appears out of the old words.

Open Tessera

How it works

  • Break it up. Split your text into single words, whole lines, or random 2–4 word fragments (the cut-up technique).
  • Compose. Drag, tap, or use the keyboard to place tiles. Add punctuation from an unlimited palette, start new lines and stanzas, lock tiles in place, or switch to free-placement "fridge magnet" mode.
  • Listen to it. Tessera counts syllables, marks stresses, finds rhyme schemes and recognizes more than 100 poetic forms (haiku, sonnets, villanelles, limericks, ghazals, Welsh and Irish forms), live as you write. It can read your poem aloud or click out its meter.
  • Aim for a form. Choose a haiku, limerick or sonnet and get a line-by-line checklist, with the words in your bank that rhyme or fit highlighted.
  • Play. Try a blackout poem, a daily prompt, an Oulipo N+7 swap, or a constraint like a lipogram or a six-word story.
  • Keep and share. Save poems to your Library, share a link (the poem travels inside the link), export a picture or Markdown, or print.

Private by design

There are no accounts and no server. Your poems are saved only in your own browser. The only network requests are the app files themselves, the optional public-domain poem search (PoetryDB), and the optional handwritten and typewriter fonts (Google Fonts). Once loaded, Tessera works offline and can be installed like an app.
