Tessera

Every word a tile. Every poem a mosaic.
  1. Paste any text in the Paste panel (or Try a sample), then Break into words or Break into lines. Every word becomes a tile in the bank.
  2. Drag tiles onto the canvas to compose. On a phone, tap a tile and then tap where it should go. Drop between words to insert, drop well below the last line to start a new line, and drag a tile off the canvas to send it back to the bank. To move several at once, Shift-click them (or drag a box around them on the canvas) and drag the group.
  3. Shape it. Right-click (or long-press) a tile to edit, duplicate, lock or recapitalize it. Grab punctuation from the unlimited palette, use Aim for a form to get a checklist, and try Match source meter to compose into the syllable and rhyme scaffold of your source text.
Your work saves automatically in this browser. Use the Library to keep several poems, and Share to send one as a link. As you write, Tessera counts syllables and recognizes 100+ poetic forms, from haiku to villanelles. Press ? for keyboard shortcuts.
