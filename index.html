Worder
Take words apart. Put them back together, differently.
  1. Paste any text in the left column, then Break into words (or Break into lines). Every word becomes a tile in the bank.
  2. Drag tiles onto the canvas to compose. Drop between words to insert, drop well below the last line to start a new stanza, and drag a tile off the canvas to send it back to the bank.
  3. Shape it. Double-click any tile to edit it, grab punctuation from the unlimited palette, and try Match source meter to compose into the syllable and rhyme scaffold of your source text.
Your work saves automatically in this browser — close the tab and pick up where you left off. As you write, Worder counts syllables and recognizes 100+ poetic forms, from haiku to villanelles.
