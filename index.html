Tessera
Every word a tile. Every poem a mosaic.
  1. Paste any text in the Paste panel, then Break into words (or Break into lines). Every word becomes a tile in the bank.
  2. Drag tiles onto the canvas to compose. Drop between words to insert, drop well below the last line to start a new stanza, and drag a tile off the canvas to send it back to the bank. To move several at once, Shift-click them (or drag a box around them on the canvas; long-press on touch) and drag the group.
  3. Shape it. Double-click any tile to edit it, grab punctuation from the unlimited palette, and try Match source meter to compose into the syllable and rhyme scaffold of your source text.
Your work saves automatically in this browser — close the tab and pick up where you left off. As you write, Tessera counts syllables and recognizes 100+ poetic forms, from haiku to villanelles.
